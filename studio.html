Sprites

Every sprite in the registry (js/sprites.js) in the poses the game uses. A badge shows when art/art.js overrides one; the states are the names an image override can give per state.

Boards

Live boards drawn by the game's renderer with the current theme and art. The gallery rooms (tools/gallery.js) show every tool and object at once.

Tokens

The theme (js/theme.js): every colour the page and the canvas use. Edits apply live here only; copy the result into the theme section of art/art.js to keep it.

Art in use

What the manifest overrides and whether its files loaded. Edit art/art.js (or art/<name>/art.js, opened with ?art=<name>) and reload.
