Sprites

Every sprite in the registry (js/sprites.js) in the poses the game uses. A badge shows when your art.js overrides one. Under each picture: its label and, after the dot, the state name an image override uses for it (out, not "blown out"). "Tile and boxes" shows where image layers go.

Boards

Live boards drawn by the game's renderer with the current colours, speeds and art. The gallery rooms (tools/gallery.js) show every tool and object at once; they exist only here. Any level opens in the game with the link below the boards.

Colours

The theme (js/theme.js): every colour the page and the canvas use, light and dark. Click a swatch or type a value (#e2898b, rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2), tomato): everything repaints as you type. Your edits stay in this browser until you save them: Download art.js and save it over art/art.js, then reload.

Speeds and sizes

How fast things move and how big a few things are. They go in the tuning section of art.js (Download art.js includes them). The preview and the boards show the result.

Art in use

What your art.js changes, whether its files loaded, and every mistake found in it, with a hint. Edit art/art.js (or art/<name>/art.js, opened with ?art=<name>) and reload.
